## SUMMARY: What Wows vs What's Missing

### ✅ WOW Factors We Already Have
1. **Only app combining AI coaching + mental health + wearable integration + behavioral repair** — no competitor has this
2. **Persona-aware coaching** — 4 distinct voices that adapt to user state (unique)
3. **Emotional experience mapping** — fitness-specific mental health UX (unique)
4. **Behavioral repair loops** — comeback paths for missed sessions (unique)
5. **Cinematic design** — Higgsfield AI imagery, particle effects, breathing animations
6. **114 exercise library** with injury-aware recommender
7. **Unified wellness score** — physical + mental + behavioral (unique)
8. **Adherence-neutral design** — no shame, no guilt, body-neutral (MacroFactor quality)

### 🎯 WOW Opportunities (Build These Next)
1. **Whoop-style recovery circle** — the iconic 0-100% color-coded ring
2. **Apple-style activity rings** — concentric arcs showing daily movement
3. **Progress photos with before/after comparison** — Hevy's killer feature
4. **Streak celebration animation** — Duolingo-style dopamine hit
5. **Coach personality evolution** — coach adapts its style over 30 days (unique)
6. **Personalized plan reveal with celebration** — onboarding wow moment
7. **Animated onboarding progress bar** — Calm-quality smooth transitions

8. Progress photos with comparison
